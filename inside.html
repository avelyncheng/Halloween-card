Happy Halloween!


(Not written to anyone in particular,

So don't take offense whatsoever.)

Roses are red,
Violets are blue,
You smell like a stinky shoe,
But Happy Halloween to you too.


Although you smell like a stinky shoe,
You deserve a blessing too.
May your spooky night be full of treats
And laundry day bring you nice clean sheets.
I hope you take a shower tonight
before you eat your Mike and Ikes.
